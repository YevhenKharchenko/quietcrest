About the Game

In Dragon Tomb: The Spirit Attack, you'll find yourself deep in these creepy underground tombs where ancient spirits don't want you there. As explorers move deeper underground, they must avoid attacks, navigate traps, and discover hidden relics protected by supernatural forces. Each room throws something different at you - you never know what's waiting around the corner.

Gameplay Experience

You'll need fast reflexes and a keen eye to make it through alive.

  • Explore underground tomb corridors

  • Avoid mystical traps and hazards

  • Escape spirit attacks and moving obstacles

  • Collect relics and hidden treasures

  • Reach the exit to unlock the next tomb area
